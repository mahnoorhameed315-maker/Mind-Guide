🧠 MindGuide

Understand Yourself. Reflect. Grow.

Hi, I'm Mahnoor 👋

I am a Psychology student interested in understanding human emotions, thoughts, behavior, and psychological well-being.

I created MindGuide as a psychology-based self-reflection project. It gives people a simple space to reflect on their emotions, stress, sleep, motivation, concentration, and everyday well-being.

Answer the questions honestly and MindGuide will provide general suggestions based on your responses.

Important: MindGuide is designed for self-reflection and psychological awareness. It is not a diagnostic test and does not replace professional psychological or medical support.

🧠 Self-Reflection Assessment

Answer each question based on how you have been feeling recently. Choose the answer that fits you best.

1. How have you been feeling recently?

2. How would you describe your stress level?

3. How well have you been sleeping?

4. How often do you feel tired during the day?

5. How difficult is it for you to concentrate?

6. How often do you worry about things?

7. How motivated have you felt recently?

8. How often do you feel overwhelmed by your responsibilities?

9. How comfortable are you talking about your feelings?

10. How connected do you feel to people around you?

11. How often do you have difficulty relaxing?

12. How do you usually handle stressful situations?

13. How satisfied are you with your daily routine?

14. How often do you do things that you enjoy?

15. How would you describe your overall well-being?

🧠 How MindGuide Works

1️⃣ Answer

Answer 15 simple questions about your recent emotions, stress, sleep, motivation, concentration, and daily well-being.

2️⃣ Reflect

Your responses are organized into different areas of psychological well-being to help you understand where you may need more attention.

3️⃣ Understand

MindGuide explains the areas that may be affecting your everyday experience and highlights your main area of focus.

4️⃣ Improve

You receive simple, practical suggestions that may help you develop healthier everyday habits and coping strategies.
